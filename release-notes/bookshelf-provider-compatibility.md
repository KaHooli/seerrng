BookshelfNG can merge optional Google Books, Library of Congress, and Apify Goodreads-compatible results with Hardcover. SeerrNG opens those provider-specific results and carries their identity into requests, while matching existing media by ISBN when available. Configure runtime sources in BookshelfNG; Google Books needs a key, and Apify may charge.
